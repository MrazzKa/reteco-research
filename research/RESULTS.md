## 6. E2 — explicit-date reranking (train): negative

Fixed rules over the BM25 top-100, no tuning: promote documents whose stated years fall inside the query's year span ±1 — across the whole list (`hard`), inside the top 20 or top 10, or fused with the BM25 order by RRF.

| variant | macro nDCG@10 | Δ vs BM25 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| BM25 (no reranking) | 0.1005 | — |
| hard | 0.0909 | -0.0096 |
| top20 | 0.0945 | -0.0060 |
| top10 | 0.0971 | -0.0034 |
| rrf | 0.0946 | -0.0059 |

Every variant loses, in every domain. Last week's feasibility check compared gold passages with *random* corpus documents (41% vs 11% year overlap); among BM25's topically similar top-100 the year signal no longer separates gold from non-gold. A date-aware system would have to use the signal inside the ranking function or in a recall stage, not as a post-hoc reorder.
